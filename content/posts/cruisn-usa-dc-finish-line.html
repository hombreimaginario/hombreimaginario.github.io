Most of the work in the last few weeks came from playing the game over and over. The music, for a start, sounded far too quiet. Next to Tiger Road and Shadow Warrior it was 10 to 19 dB lower, because the sound bank was scaled to the loudest sound effect and the game runs at its factory volume, 200 out of 255. Race music is now 6 to 10 dB louder, and that factory setting counts as the maximum. Crashes had already been turned down 4.4 dB after a tester said they were too loud.

Attract mode was missing two things from the arcade: the 3D Midway logo between the high scores and the demo race, and the rotating car after it. The logo is made of flat-shaded polygons that the port was throwing away (long story short, I had screwed up). The car never showed up because the demo race lasted 30 seconds, the lighter tracks I use in the port run out of scenery after about 22, and the game's watchdog restarted everything. On a real console that meant 48 seconds of frozen picture, as the same tester reported. The demo race now ends on a real-time clock. The logo spins for a couple of seconds, and the music starts with it and keeps playing through the race.

In the last post I said attract mode picked its tracks at random. It turned out it didn't quite: it chose from only three, and it always started with the same one, because the time since boot was almost identical every time the console was switched on. The draw now also uses the console clock, and the demo picks from six tracks (Arizona, US 101, Grand Canyon, Redwood Forest, Appalachia and Iowa), never the same one twice in a row. The rotating car is random too. In the arcade it always came in the same order.

Menus are nicer with the d-pad. On the car and track screens, and when entering your initials, the selection no longer springs back to the centre when you let go, just like the arcade wheel. With the Dreamcast racing wheel, which has no X button, B now toggles the hidden cars and tracks and changes the radio station.

There's also another round of small performance work. A few more routines of the original program now run as native code, distant cars use simpler models, polygons smaller than a pixel are skipped, and the translated code takes about 840 KB less RAM.

Version 0.9.2 is now with the testers. There isn't much left on my list, and I think the project is close to done. You can follow me on X for more updates.
