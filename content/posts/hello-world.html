This is where I'll be posting progress on my retro works: ports of arcade and console games to the Mega Drive, Saturn, Dreamcast and Neo Geo, plus whatever reverse engineering they need along the way.

Expect short updates rather than polished articles: what I tried, what worked, what didn't, and a few screenshots. Use the platform buttons on the home page or the archive to follow a single system.

The site itself is plain static HTML on GitHub Pages, with no database behind it. If you'd rather not check back, there's an RSS feed.
