Cruis'n USA: Arcade Edition

Midway · 1994 · arcade

The arcade game, not the N64 one: Midway's V-Unit program translated into native Dreamcast code, with its video and sound hardware replaced by the PowerVR and the AICA, Dreamcast controls, VMU saves and the hidden cars and tracks on a button.
